# Skill: Backlog Refinement

## Purpose

Review, prioritize, and refine the product backlog. Keeps work items clear, current, and properly ordered.

## When to Use

- Before sprint or iteration planning
- When the backlog is stale or disorganized
- User asks to "clean up the backlog"

## Required Inputs

- Current backlog items
- Stakeholder priorities and business context

## Workflow

1. Review each backlog item for clarity and completeness
2. Verify acceptance criteria exist for each item
3. Prioritize based on business value and dependencies
4. Remove or archive items that are no longer relevant
5. Split items that are too large (use `story-splitting` skill)
6. Document updated backlog

## Output

A refined, prioritized backlog with clear, actionable items.

## Forbidden

- Deleting items without confirming with stakeholders
- Changing priorities without justification
- Merging unrelated items
