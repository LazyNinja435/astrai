# Skill: Stakeholder Summary

## Purpose

Translate technical work into business-friendly summaries for non-technical stakeholders. Communicates what was done, why it matters, and what's next.

## When to Use

- After completing a feature or significant change
- Before a stakeholder demo or review
- User asks "what should I tell the business?"

## Required Inputs

- Completed work details (from handoff or PR)
- Business context and priorities

## Workflow

1. Review the completed work
2. Translate technical details into business outcomes
3. Highlight value delivered
4. Note any risks or tradeoffs in business terms
5. Summarize next steps

## Output

A stakeholder-friendly summary (2-3 paragraphs) of what was done and why it matters.

## Forbidden

- Using technical jargon without explanation
- Overpromising or misrepresenting status
- Omitting risks that stakeholders need to know
