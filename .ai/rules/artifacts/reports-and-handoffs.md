# Reports and Handoffs

## Rule: Report What Matters

Reports should include:
- What was done and why
- What was not done (and why)
- Known issues or risks
- Next steps

## Rule: Use Handoff Template

For task handoffs, use `.ai/templates/handoff.template.md`. Include:
- Summary of completed work
- Files changed with descriptions
- Test results
- Risks and caveats
- Next steps

## Rule: Objective, Not Subjective

Reports should state facts, not opinions. "Test suite passes with 42 tests" not "The code looks good."

## Rule: No Blame

Handoff documents describe what was done and what remains. Do not assign blame or criticize prior work.
