# Protocol: Requirement to Story

## Purpose

Transform product requirements into actionable user stories with acceptance criteria.

## Trigger

Requirements are confirmed and approved.

## Flow

```
Requirements Confirmed
    │
    ▼
[1] Review → Requirements document
    │
    ▼
[2] Story Writing → Skill: product-owner/requirement-discovery
    │
    ▼
[3] Acceptance Criteria → Skill: product-owner/acceptance-criteria
    │                        Template: acceptance-criteria.template.md
    │
    ▼
[4] Story Splitting → If too large: product-owner/story-splitting
    │
    ▼
[5] Backlog → Add to backlog
```

## Related

- Skills: `product-owner/`
- Templates: `acceptance-criteria.template.md`
