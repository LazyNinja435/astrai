# Protocol: Backlog Refinement Flow

## Purpose

Review, prioritize, and refine the product backlog.

## Trigger

Before sprint/iteration planning or when backlog is stale.

## Flow

```
Backlog Exists
    │
    ▼
[1] Review → Each item for clarity and completeness
    │
    ▼
[2] Prioritize → By business value and dependencies
    │
    ▼
[3] Split → Large items: product-owner/story-splitting
    │
    ▼
[4] Remove → Stale or irrelevant items (with confirmation)
    │
    ▼
[5] Document → Updated backlog
```
